Privacy

Last updated: 2026

Aubade is built to be private by default. You don't need an account to listen, and your library, likes, playlists and listening history are stored locally on your device — not on our servers.

What we store on your device

  • Your playlists, liked songs and recently played tracks.
  • Your playback preferences (audio quality, theming, volume).
  • A small cache of artwork and audio so the app works offline.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell your data.
  • We don't require personal information to use the player.
  • We don't show ads.

Music metadata and audio are provided by third-party catalog services. When optional cloud sync launches, it will be strictly opt-in and clearly explained before you enable it.
