About Octave

Music should just feel good.

Octave started as a simple idea: a music player that’s genuinely beautiful, completely free, and respects the person using it. No ads stitched between songs. No paywall on the parts that matter. Just your music, presented with care.

01

Built around the song

Every screen puts the music first. The player takes its colour from the album, lyrics keep time word by word, and it stays fast on a laptop or a phone. The kind of care you’d expect from an app you pay for.

02

Free, and staying that way

Listening shouldn’t cost anything, and there are no ads to make up for it. Accounts come through our Discord now — that’s what keeps scrapers out and the servers paid for. Your library, likes and playlists come with you.

03

Made by people who love music

Octave is a small, independent project, rebuilt from the ground up in 2026 with a stubborn focus on craft, speed and design. The roadmap is mostly the Discord’s requests.
